You built the cage yourself — one achievement at a time.

The identity cage isn't imposed on healthcare professionals from the outside. It's assembled from the inside — medical school acceptance letter by board exam by clinical credential by title by mortgage. Each milestone added another bar.

Five signs you're inside one:

1. You can't describe who you are without using your job title
2. The thought of leaving medicine triggers shame, not just sadness
3. You've made major life decisions based on what a physician "should" do
4. You feel like a fraud in any room where nobody knows your credentials
5. You stay in a role that's hurting you because leaving would mean betraying who you are

**You are not betraying your past self by evolving. You are honoring them.**

The credential doesn't own you. The identity is a story, and stories can be revised.
