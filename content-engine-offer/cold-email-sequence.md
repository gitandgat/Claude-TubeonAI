# Cold Email Sequence — Competitor Content Engine

The free sample does the selling. The email's only job is to get them to open it.
Peer-to-peer, specific, no vendor-speak. Send from a real person, a warmed domain.

## Merge variables (filled from the pipeline)
- `{first}` — prospect first name
- `{king}` — their category king (parasite target)
- `{finding}` — the one-line hook from their gap report (e.g. Patrick Dang's drift)
- `{teaser_url}` — link to their hosted gap report (or attach the 1-pager)
- `{their_topic}` — e.g. "cold calling", "system design", "resume"

---

## Email 1 — the sample (Day 0)

**Subject:** {king} is leaving you content
*(alt: "made you something" · "your {their_topic} gap report")*

> Hey {first},
>
> I went through {king}'s last 25 videos and yours, and noticed {finding}.
>
> So I built you a one-page content gap report — 5 posts pulled straight from where
> they're winning and you're not. No opt-in, no catch, it's right here: {teaser_url}
>
> I run a little engine that does this every week for {their_topic} coaches. If the
> report's useful I'll tell you how it works. If not, no worries — keep the report.
>
> — [name]

Rules: under 90 words. The link is the CTA. No calendar link in email 1.

---

## Email 2 — the proof nudge (Day 3, reply to Email 1)

**Subject:** (re: same thread)

> Quick one — did the gap report land? The {finding} angle is the one I'd run first;
> it's the kind of post that pulls their audience to you instead.
>
> Happy to build the full month (≈25 pieces, scheduled) so you see it working before
> you commit to anything. Worth 15 min?

---

## Email 3 — the breakup (Day 7, reply)

**Subject:** (re: same thread)

> I'll stop here so I'm not cluttering your inbox, {first}.
>
> If content's just not the priority right now, all good. If it is and you'd rather not
> do it yourself, the report shows what I'd ship. Door's open whenever.
>
> — [name]

---

## Principles (don't break these)
1. **The free sample is the whole pitch.** The email is a wrapper. If the report is weak,
   no subject line saves it — run client-facing reports through Claude / `/stop-slop`.
2. **One specific {finding} per prospect.** Reusing the same line across the list = it
   reads as a blast and dies. The generator produces a different gap per prospect — use it.
3. **No links in the body except the report.** (Your own standing rule: link in the
   report/first comment, not the cold open.)
4. **Volume target:** 20–30/day on a warmed domain, 2 follow-ups each.
   Math from the plan: ~400 sends → ~30 replies → 5–10 calls → 1–3 closes.
5. **Never fabricate the {finding}.** It comes from the real mined data, every time.
