[POINT 1 — 3-12s]
[VISUAL: quick cut, direct to camera]

But you've been saying that for years.

Try this instead: traffic light your habits.

High achievers are good at looking fine.
That's the trap.
If you don't tell someone you're struggling — you delay your own recovery.
Talk to your doctor. Reach out. You were not built to carry this by yourself.
