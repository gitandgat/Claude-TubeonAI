---

When I walked away from medicine, there was no AI to sit with at 2am.

No tool to ask the questions I was too afraid to ask out loud. No system to help me translate 12 years of clinical training into a language the rest of the world could understand.

There was just me, a lot of spiraling, and eventually — a crossing guard vest I didn't expect to teach me anything.

The crossing took two years.

Now I work with burned-out nurses, physicians, and NPs who are standing where I stood. And the thing I keep noticing is this: the clarity that took me two years to find — they're getting it in 30 minutes.

Not because they're smarter than I was. Because they have tools I didn't.

These are the 5 prompts I give every client at the start of our work together:

→ The Identity Excavator — who are you beneath the job title?
→ The Skills Translator — your clinical skills in non-clinical language
→ The Financial Fear Solver — the math that makes "I can't leave" feel less true
→ The Values Compass — what you're running toward, not just away from
→ The Decision Defogged — the case for and against, and what a wise mentor would say

I put all 5 in a free guide. Every prompt is copy-paste ready for Claude or ChatGPT.

I'm a little jealous my clients get to use these. I wish they'd existed when I was crossing over.
