[Slide 1]
Self-doubt is not a verdict.
It's a signal you're early.

[Slide 2]
Your nervous system isn't scanning for truth.
It scans for familiarity — and flags anything unfamiliar as threat. Even when that "threat" is growth.

[Slide 3]
Confidence doesn't come before action.
Your brain grants it after. It needs proof — not encouragement. One sentence. One post. One decision made without waiting.

[Slide 4]
Self-doubt lives in a gap.
The gap between what you're doing and who you still think you are. Shrink the gap. The doubt loses its hold.

[Slide 5]
The 24-hour rule.
When self-doubt shows up, take one next step within 24 hours. Not the full plan. Just the next visible move. Self-doubt is like milk — leave it too long and it hardens.

[Slide 6]
The goal is not to eliminate self-doubt.
It's to outgrow it. To build an identity where that particular fear no longer makes behavioral sense.
