20,000 immigrant doctors in Canada.
32,000 immigrant nurses.

All qualified. All here. None of them allowed to practice.

Not because they're incompetent.
Not because there's no need.

Because of licensing barriers. Because credentials don't transfer. Because a system designed to protect the gate moves slower than the crisis it's supposed to serve.

And yet 52,000 trained healthcare professionals sit on the sidelines while emergency rooms close and wait times grow.

The barrier wasn't competence.
