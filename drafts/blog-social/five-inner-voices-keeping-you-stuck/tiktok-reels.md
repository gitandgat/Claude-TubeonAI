# TikTok / Reels Script (45-60 seconds)

## Hook (0-3s)
voiceover: "Your inner critic has a name. And naming it changes everything."
on_screen_text: "Name the voice."
visual_note: Close-up to camera, calm and direct.

## Scene 1 — The Problem (3-15s)
voiceover: "Research shows 90 to 95 percent of senior leaders — at Google, McKinsey, JP Morgan — carry self-doubt. It doesn't go away when you get promoted. It gets louder. And in healthcare? The culture trains these voices for decades."
on_screen_text: "Self-doubt scales up."
visual_note: Walk and talk, or slow pan on a corridor.

## Scene 2 — The Insight (15-30s)
voiceover: "Researchers found five recurring voices. The Judge says you're not enough. The Ringmaster says one more win will finally make you worthy — then moves the target. The Neglector says your needs come last. Sound familiar?"
on_screen_text: "5 inner voices."
visual_note: Text list appears on screen, one by one.

## Scene 3 — The Takeaway (30-45s)
voiceover: "The fix isn't silencing them. It's separating from them. When a critical thought shows up, name it. Shift it: instead of 'I am not enough,' say 'The Judge is telling me I'm not enough.' Then: 'I see you. But no thanks.' That gap is where your power lives."
on_screen_text: "Name it. Reject it."
visual_note: Talking to camera, deliberate pace.

## CTA (45-55s)
voiceover: "Full article at Crosswalk Wisdom dot com — link in bio."
on_screen_text: "Read the full article ^"
visual_note: Smile, point to screen.
