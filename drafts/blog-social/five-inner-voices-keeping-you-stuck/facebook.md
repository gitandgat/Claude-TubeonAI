Something I keep coming back to: self-doubt doesn't disappear when you earn the title. It gets louder.

Researchers who work with senior leaders at Google, McKinsey, and JP Morgan found that 90-95% of high achievers carry meaningful self-doubt. And they identified five recurring inner voices behind it — The Judge, The Victimizer, The Misguided Protector, The Ringmaster, and The Neglector. Healthcare professionals tend to carry all five, trained by a culture that punishes uncertainty and rewards relentless output.

The practice that actually helps isn't silencing these voices. It's learning to name them and step back. When the Judge shows up, you say: "The Judge is telling me I'm not enough" — not "I am not enough." That small shift in language creates psychological distance. And distance is where your agency lives.
