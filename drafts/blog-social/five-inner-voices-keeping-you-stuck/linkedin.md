# LinkedIn Post

## Hook
90-95% of senior leaders at Google, McKinsey, and JP Morgan experience self-doubt.

## Tension
It doesn't disappear when you get promoted.

It scales with responsibility.

## Body
Researchers studied thousands of professionals and found the same five inner voices — what they call "inner deceivers" — showing up again and again:

The Judge: "You're not enough."
The Victimizer: "Nothing will change."
The Misguided Protector: "Stay small. It's safer."
The Ringmaster: "One more win and you'll finally feel worthy."
The Neglector: "Everyone else comes first."

Healthcare attracts Ringmaster-dominant people. The culture rewards them. And then it grinds them down.

The breakthrough isn't silencing these voices. It's learning to separate from them.

When one shows up, name it. "Classic Judge." Then shift the language: instead of "I am not enough," say "The Judge is telling me I'm not enough."

That gap between the thought and your response is where your power lives.

## Key Takeaway
You are not your thoughts. You never were.
