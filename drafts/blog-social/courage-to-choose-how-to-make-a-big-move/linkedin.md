You don't have to burn everything down to build something new.

The narrative around career change is often dramatic: quit cold, burn the boat, leap before you look. It makes great content. It makes terrible advice for a 42-year-old with a mortgage, three kids, and $300K in student loans.

There's a different path: build the bridge before you burn the boat.

Start with one informational interview per week. Dedicate two hours on Sunday to building your new identity online. Keep the income flowing while you map the terrain. Test before you commit. Every successful transition I've seen in healthcare follows this structure — not a dramatic leap, but a series of deliberate, compounding small bets.

**You don't have to choose between your current life and your next life. You architect the transition.**

The courage to choose isn't the courage to blow everything up. It's the courage to build something different while still standing in the old structure — knowing when the new one is ready, and stepping across.
