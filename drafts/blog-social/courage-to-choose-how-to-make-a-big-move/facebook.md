You don't have to burn everything down to build something new.

The "burn the boat" narrative makes great content and terrible advice for a 42-year-old with a mortgage and student loans. Build the bridge before you burn the boat: one informational interview per week, two hours Sunday building your new presence online, keep the income flowing while you map the terrain. Test before you commit. Every successful healthcare career transition I've seen follows this structure — not a dramatic leap, but a series of deliberate, compounding small bets.
