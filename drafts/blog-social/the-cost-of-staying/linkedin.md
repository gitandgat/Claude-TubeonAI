Nearly half of all physicians are burned out. The question isn't whether you can afford to leave.

The real question is: can you afford to stay?

48.2% of physicians report burnout. Physicians die by suicide at 1.5-2x the rate of the general population. Burned-out clinicians are 171% more likely to report relationship dissatisfaction. These aren't soft statistics. They're the hidden cost of staying in a role that's slowly erasing you.

We talk about the financial risk of leaving. We rarely calculate the financial — and human — cost of staying. Chronic burnout doesn't just affect your mood. It affects your judgment, your relationships, your lifespan, your kids.

**The most dangerous assumption in medicine: that the safest path is the one you're already on.**

Burnout isn't a personal failure. It's a structural signal. And ignoring it is its own kind of risk.
