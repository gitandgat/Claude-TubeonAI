You will never have enough information to eliminate uncertainty. That's not the goal.

The goal is a good enough framework for making decisions you can live with.

Three that work:

**1. The Regret Minimization Framework (Bezos)**
Project yourself to age 80. Which decision would you regret more — trying and failing, or never trying at all? Jeff Bezos used this to leave Wall Street and build Amazon. He bet on regret over risk.

**2. The Pre-Mortem**
Assume the decision fails. Now work backward: why did it fail? This surfaces the real risks — not the imagined ones — and lets you address them before you act.

**3. The Reversibility Test**
Is this decision reversible? Most decisions we treat as permanent are actually reversible. When you recognize a decision can be undone, the fear loses half its weight.

Research shows people regret inactions far more than actions over the long term. The decision that haunts you most at 80 is usually the one you didn't make.

**The goal isn't to eliminate uncertainty. It's to make decisions you can live with.**
