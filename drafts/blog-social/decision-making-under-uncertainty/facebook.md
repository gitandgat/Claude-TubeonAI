You will never have enough information to eliminate uncertainty. That's not the goal.

Three frameworks that actually work for big decisions: Regret Minimization (the question Bezos used to leave Wall Street and start Amazon), the Pre-Mortem (assume failure, work backward to the real risks), and the Reversibility Test (most decisions we treat as permanent are actually reversible).

Research shows people regret inactions far more than actions over the long term. The decision haunting you at 80 is usually the one you didn't make.
