# Facebook Post — Beyond the White Coat

---

Leaving medicine doesn't feel like a career change. It feels like losing yourself.

That's not in your head. Research shows that physicians fuse their identity with their profession so completely that considering a career transition triggers genuine psychological threat. The white coat becomes who you are — and stepping away from it feels like stepping away from yourself.

What helped me see it differently: you don't have to leave your identity. You expand it. Every skill you built in medicine — reading a room, communicating in a crisis, thinking in systems — those travel with you, wherever you go. They don't belong to the hospital. They belong to you.
