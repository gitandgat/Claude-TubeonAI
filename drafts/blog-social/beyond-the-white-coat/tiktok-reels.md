# TikTok / Reels Script — Beyond the White Coat

**Total runtime: ~50 seconds**

---

**Hook (0–3s)**
voiceover: "Leaving medicine doesn't feel like a career change. It feels like dying."
on_screen_text: "Leaving medicine feels like dying."
visual_note: Talking head, direct eye contact, neutral background or clinical setting

---

**Scene 1 (3–18s)**
voiceover: "And there's actually research behind that. Physicians fuse their personal identity with their profession so deeply that considering a career change triggers what researchers call identity threat. You're not being dramatic. The white coat literally becomes part of who you are. Your title becomes inseparable from your name."
on_screen_text: "It's called identity threat."
visual_note: Talking head, slower pace, serious tone

---

**Scene 2 (18–35s)**
voiceover: "A 2022 study found that 67% of physicians considering a career change were more afraid of losing social status than losing income. Add 200,000 dollars in debt and 15 years of sacrifice — and staying, even when it's destroying you, starts to feel safer than leaving."
on_screen_text: "67% fear losing status more than income."
visual_note: Talking head, lean in slightly — this is the gut punch moment

---

**Scene 3 (35–48s)**
voiceover: "But here's the reframe. You don't have to leave your identity. You expand it. Pattern recognition. Emotional resilience. Communication under pressure. These aren't medical skills — they're human skills. And they're exactly what the rest of the world is looking for."
on_screen_text: "You don't leave. You expand."
visual_note: Energy shifts — more hopeful, sit up, slight smile

---

**CTA (48–55s)**
voiceover: "Full article at Crosswalk Wisdom dot com. Link in bio."
on_screen_text: "Link in bio 👆"
visual_note: Point up, end frame with website URL on screen
