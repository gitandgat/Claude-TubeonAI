He didn't wait until he felt ready. He put on the vest and walked into the intersection.

A traffic controller at a busy construction site — no working lights, no infrastructure. Just a vest, a hand signal, and the authority he chose to claim. Cars stopped. Traffic flowed. Not because conditions were perfect. Because he moved.

Most career transitions fail in the waiting room. Waiting for certainty, the right time, permission that nobody is coming to grant. Clarity comes from movement, not from waiting.
