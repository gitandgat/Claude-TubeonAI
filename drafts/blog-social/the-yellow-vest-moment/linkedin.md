He didn't wait until he felt ready. He put on the vest and walked into the intersection.

The traffic controller at a busy construction site. No lights. No infrastructure. Just a vest, a hand, and the authority he chose to claim. Cars stopped. Traffic flowed. Because he acted — not because he felt certain.

That's the yellow vest moment. The instant you stop waiting for conditions to be perfect and step into the intersection anyway.

Most career transitions fail in the waiting room. We wait for certainty that never arrives, for the right time that keeps receding, for permission that nobody is coming to grant.

**Clarity comes from movement, not from waiting.**

Every physician and nurse I've spoken to who successfully pivoted has their yellow vest moment — the specific day they stopped researching and started moving. Not ready. Moving anyway.

The vest is available. The intersection is there. The question is whether you'll step in.
