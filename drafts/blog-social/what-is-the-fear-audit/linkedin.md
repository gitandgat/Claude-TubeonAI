Most people know they're afraid. They just don't know what they're actually afraid of.

Fear of failure and fear of success feel identical in your nervous system. Fear of judgment and fear of financial ruin show up the same way — racing heart, avoidance, endless research that never leads to action.

The Fear Audit is a structured tool that does one thing: it names the fear specifically. Not "I'm scared" but "I'm afraid of losing my colleagues' respect" or "I'm afraid my family will see me as irresponsible."

Because once you name it precisely, it changes shape. The vague dread becomes a specific concern. And specific concerns can be addressed.

**Naming the fear doesn't make it go away. It puts you back in the driver's seat.**

I built the Fear Audit after watching hundreds of healthcare professionals stay stuck in careers that were destroying them — not because they lacked information, but because they couldn't see clearly what was actually holding them back.

The audit is free. The clarity it generates isn't.
