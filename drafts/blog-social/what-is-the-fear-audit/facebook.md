Most people know they're afraid. They just don't know what they're actually afraid of.

Fear of judgment and fear of financial ruin show up the same way — racing heart, avoidance, research that never becomes action. The Fear Audit names the fear specifically. Not "I'm scared" but "I'm afraid of losing my colleagues' respect." Named fears change shape. Vague dread becomes something you can actually address.

I built it because the most common reason healthcare professionals stay stuck isn't information. It's clarity about what's actually holding them back.
