The safety net you're holding onto has holes in it.

Here's what the data actually shows about "job security" in medicine:

- Average job tenure in the US: 4.1 years — not a career, a chapter
- 77% of physicians are now employed, not independent — they work for systems, not themselves
- 90+ health systems announced layoffs in 2023 alone
- Physician purchasing power has declined 29% since 2001

The story we were sold — stay in medicine, collect a salary, retire at 65 — is increasingly fiction. The hospital that employs you is a corporation with shareholders, margins, and an exit strategy that doesn't include you.

**Build a trampoline instead. Individual leverage is the new job security.**

The safest position in an uncertain world isn't employment. It's optionality. Diverse income, portable skills, a personal brand that travels with you — these are the new moats.
