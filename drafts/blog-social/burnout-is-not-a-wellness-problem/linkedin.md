No amount of self-care will fix a structural problem.

The wellness industry sold healthcare workers a very convenient lie: that burnout is a personal failure, addressable with better habits, more resilience, the right morning routine. Yoga mats were distributed. Resilience workshops were scheduled. Nothing changed.

Burnout isn't a wellness problem. It's an identity crisis — a collision between who you were trained to be and who you can no longer afford to remain.

The solution isn't another self-care checklist. It's asking a harder question: is the role I'm in still aligned with who I am?

**Burnout is your life asking for a renegotiation.**

That's not comfortable. But it's honest. And honest is where transformation actually begins.
