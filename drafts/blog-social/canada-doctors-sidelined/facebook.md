# Facebook — canada-doctors-sidelined

There's a statistic I haven't been able to shake.

Canada has 20,000 trained immigrant physicians and 32,000 immigrant nurses who are fully qualified to practice medicine — and aren't allowed to. Not because they failed an exam. Not because there's no need. Because the licensing system hasn't caught up with reality.

Meanwhile, emergency rooms are closing. Healthcare workers inside the system are leaving in record numbers. And 52,000 people who could help are waiting.

I wrote about what I think this reveals — not just as a policy failure, but as a mirror for every healthcare professional who has felt invisible inside the very institution they gave everything to.

Because the dynamic is the same. A credentialing committee can't see your clinical depth. A staffing grid can't see your burnout. And when the system keeps proving it can't quite see you, it's very easy to start believing the system is right.

It isn't.
