INSTAGRAM CAPTION:

Your brain built an avoidance loop — and it's been rewarding itself for years.

Every time you thought about the pivot, felt dread, and closed the tab, your brain filed that as: avoidance works. Repeat. Researcher Tim Pychyl spent years studying this. His finding: procrastination isn't laziness. It's an emotion regulation failure. You're not avoiding the task — you're avoiding how you expect it will feel.

The fix is embarrassingly small. Name the emotion. Shrink the action. Start for five minutes. The dread before the crosswalk is always worse than crossing it.
