The fear of judgment is the most expensive tax you'll ever pay — and the research says the bill is mostly fictional.

Cornell's Spotlight Effect study found that people estimated 50% of their peers would notice an embarrassing moment. The actual number? 23%. We are far less visible than we think. And loss aversion means we're weighting imagined criticism at double its actual impact, then making career decisions from that inflated fear.

The colleagues you're afraid will judge you for leaving? They're worried about their own careers.
