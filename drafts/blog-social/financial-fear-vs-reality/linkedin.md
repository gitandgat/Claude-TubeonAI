Your financial fears are probably worse than your financial reality.

Fear is a bad accountant. Math is the antidote.

Most physicians' bare-minimum monthly expenses are 40-60% lower than their current spending. When you actually do the math — stripping out the lifestyle inflation, the car lease, the membership dues — the runway looks different. One physician's real calculation: 11.4 months of full runway on existing savings alone.

And that's before locum work. Locum rates run $1,500-$3,000 per day. Two weeks of locum shifts per month and you're covering most of your baseline. The pivot isn't necessarily a financial cliff. It might be a financial detour.

**The number that's terrifying you is probably not the real number. You just haven't done the actual math yet.**

Sit down with a spreadsheet before you make a fear-based decision in either direction.
