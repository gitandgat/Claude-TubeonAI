AI passed the USMLE in the 90th percentile. It still can't sit with a dying patient.

GPT-4's USMLE performance is not a threat — it's a redistribution. AI now matches physician accuracy in radiology, pathology, and dermatology interpretation. The diagnostic computing is being automated.

What isn't being automated: genuine empathy. Moral reasoning under ambiguity. Presence. The ability to hold a patient's fear without flinching. The clinical intuition that comes from 10,000 hours of looking at actual human beings.

These are not consolation prizes. They are the highest-value outputs of a medical career — and they are, by design, irreplaceable.

**The machines will do the computing. You do the caring. That's not a consolation prize — it's the highest calling.**

The physicians most at risk in an AI-integrated world are those who have only ever competed on information processing speed. The ones who are irreplaceable are those who have invested in the human skills medicine demands.
