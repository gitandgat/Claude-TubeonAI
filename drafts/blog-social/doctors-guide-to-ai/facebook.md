AI passed the USMLE in the 90th percentile. It still can't sit with a dying patient.

GPT-4 matches physician-level accuracy in radiology, pathology, and dermatology. The diagnostic computing is being automated — and healthcare professionals need to stop pretending it isn't. What AI cannot replicate: genuine empathy, moral reasoning, presence. The ability to hold a patient's fear without flinching.

The machines will do the computing. You do the caring. That is not a consolation prize — it's the highest calling.
