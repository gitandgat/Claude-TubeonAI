Your body quit before you did.

Most burned-out healthcare professionals aren't failing at self-care.

They're succeeding at the wrong habits — the ones that keep the nervous system locked in survival mode long after the shift ends.

Ten of them keep showing up again and again.

Saying yes before checking what you have left. Going to bed late to steal back "me time" through doom-scrolling. Holding pre-burnout standards against a post-burnout body. Managing it entirely alone — because high achievers are very good at looking fine.

That last one delays recovery more than any other.

If you don't tell someone what's actually happening — not the managed version, the real one — you delay your own recovery. Your body has been sending the signal for a while now. The question is what you do with it.

Burnout recovery isn't an event. It's a slow accumulation of stopped habits.
