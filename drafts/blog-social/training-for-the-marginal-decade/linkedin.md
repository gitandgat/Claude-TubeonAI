Your last decade of life is being decided right now — in your 40s.

Muscle mass declines 3-8% per decade after 30. Grip strength is the single strongest predictor of all-cause mortality — stronger than blood pressure, stronger than cholesterol. Zone 2 cardio (the kind where you can still hold a conversation) reduces all-cause mortality by approximately 50%.

We spend enormous energy building careers. We spend almost none building the bodies those careers will depend on. The irony: the healthcare professionals who know this best are often the worst offenders. We teach it. We don't do it.

**Exercise is the most potent longevity drug in existence. No pharmaceutical comes close.**

The marginal decade — your last 10 functional years — is shaped by what you do at 40, not 70. You're not training for how you feel tomorrow. You're training for who you'll be at 85.
