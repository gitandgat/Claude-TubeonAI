Not because AI does the work for you. Because it handles the cognitive load — the research rabbit holes, the draft iterations, the 2am "how do I even start" paralysis — so you can focus on the part that matters: courage.

- Drafting and refining your "why I'm leaving medicine" story
- Researching non-clinical roles and compensation benchmarks
- Preparing for informational interviews
- Building a LinkedIn profile that speaks to business, not just clinical credentials
- Synthesizing job descriptions to identify transferable skill matches

Used well, AI saves 10+ hours per week during transition. That's 10 hours redirected to conversations, decisions, and movement.

**AI is a co-pilot, not the pilot. It handles the cognitive load so you can focus on courage.**

The tools exist. The question is whether you'll use them.
