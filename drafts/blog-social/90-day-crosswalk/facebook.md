Career transitions fail for one of two reasons: people rush in before they're ready, or they research forever without ever moving.

The 90-Day Crosswalk is a three-phase roadmap: Discovery (days 1-30), Positioning (days 31-60), and Execution (days 61-90). By week 3 you're doing informational interviews. By day 60 your LinkedIn is rebuilt for a business audience. By day 61 you're applying with precision — not panic.

The reason most people stay stuck isn't lack of information. It's lack of a structure to hold the process.
