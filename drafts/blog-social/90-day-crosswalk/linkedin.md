Career transitions fail for one of two reasons: rushing in, or researching forever without acting.

The 90-Day Crosswalk is a three-phase framework built for healthcare professionals who are serious about pivoting — without blowing up their current life in the process.

**Phase 1 — Discovery (Days 1-30):** Audit your skills, clarify your non-negotiables, identify 3-5 target roles or industries. No applications yet.

**Phase 2 — Positioning (Days 31-60):** Rebuild your LinkedIn for business audiences. Complete 10 informational interviews. Draft your transition narrative.

**Phase 3 — Execution (Days 61-90):** Apply with precision. Negotiate from strength. Take the first step across.

90 days. 3 phases. One direction. That's all it takes to build clarity and momentum.

The reason most people stay stuck isn't lack of information. It's lack of a structured container to hold the process.
