The business world doesn't care that you can manage a ventilator.

They care that you can make high-stakes decisions under pressure with incomplete information. Same skill. Different language.

Healthcare professionals carry 7 transferable superpowers that the business world is actively paying for — they just don't know how to name them yet:

1. **High-stakes decision-making** — routinely operating at the limit of available information
2. **Systems thinking** — understanding how a change in one variable cascades through a complex system
3. **Rapid pattern recognition** — synthesizing ambiguous signals into actionable conclusions
4. **Stakeholder communication under pressure** — families, teams, administrators, all at once
5. **Protocol design and adherence** — creating repeatable, high-reliability processes
6. **Emotional regulation** — performing at peak while managing your own and others' distress
7. **Evidence evaluation** — knowing the difference between signal and noise

The portfolio career model — consulting + coaching + content + advisory — is where most healthcare professionals land after translation. Each role compounds the others.

**You don't need to abandon your expertise. You need to rename it.**
