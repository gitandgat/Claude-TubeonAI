Your career isn't separate from your health. It IS your health.

Job dissatisfaction predicts worse health outcomes by age 40 — not just lower morale, actual measurable deterioration. Chronically disengaged employees have 60% higher rates of depression than their engaged peers. And the research that won a Nobel Prize: Elizabeth Blackburn's telomere work shows chronic stress ages your cells by up to 10 years.

10 years. Not metaphorically. Biologically.

The healthcare system has been very good at helping us see physical and mental health as separate from professional life. The data disagrees. Your cortisol levels don't know the difference between a difficult patient and a difficult marriage. Chronic occupational stress is chronic stress — full stop.

**Making a career change isn't just a professional decision. It's a health intervention.**

If a patient came to you with chronic stress, poor sleep, low energy, and 60% higher depression risk — you'd intervene. You are that patient.
