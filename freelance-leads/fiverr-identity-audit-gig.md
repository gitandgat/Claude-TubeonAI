## About This Gig (Sahawat's copy + MD-forward opener)

I'm a former physician. I walked away from the career I'd built my entire identity around — so when a life stops fitting, I don't know it from a book. I've lived it.

Sometimes, you wake up and realize you’re wearing a life that doesn’t quite fit anymore.

Maybe you’ve checked all the "right" boxes, chased the goals you were supposed to chase, and still feel a quiet sense of disconnect. Or maybe you are standing at the edge of a major transition, trying to figure out who you are now, before you take the next step.

If you are tired of generic advice, hustle culture, and surface-level fluff, you are in the right place.

I don't offer quick fixes. I offer an Identity Audit—a thoughtful, collaborative, and grounded exploration of who you are, what you actually value, and where you need to draw the line between your own desires and other people's expectations.

**What is an Identity Audit?**
It is a dedicated space to pause, untangle, and look closely at the life you are building. Together, we will:

- **Strip away the noise:** Identify the beliefs, scripts, and expectations that belong to other people, not you.
- **Define your boundaries:** Look at where your energy is leaking and map out the boundaries necessary to protect your time and peace.
- **Find your true north:** Reconnect with what actually brings you a sense of vitality and alignment, so you can move forward with clarity and confidence.

**What to Expect from Our Sessions:**

- **A Creative Partner:** I am not here to talk down to you or hand you a one-size-fits-all worksheet. We are thought partners. I will ask the hard, necessary questions and push deeper when things get vague.
- **Radical Honesty:** Safe, non-judgmental, and deeply real conversations.
- **Grounded Clarity:** You will walk away with a sharper understanding of your current season of life and actionable clarity on your next steps.

True wellness isn't just about building better habits; it’s about making sure the life you are building is actually yours.

Let’s figure out what your next chapter looks like. Send me a message, and let's get started.

## FAQ

- **What happens after I order?** I send a few intake questions, we book a time
  that works in your timezone, and we meet by video call. You get your written
  summary after.
- **Is this therapy or counseling?** No. This is coaching — a forward-looking
  exploration of identity and direction. It isn't a substitute for mental-health
  treatment, and I'll always say so if therapy is the better fit.
- **Who is this for?** Anyone who feels their life no longer fits — especially
  professionals weighing a major transition. (I'm a former physician who's
  navigated exactly that, so career/identity shifts are home turf.)
- **What if I don't know which package to pick?** Message me first — happy to do
  a free 10-minute intro so we both know it's a fit. Or start with the Clarity
  Session.
- **Where are you based / what timezone?** Sessions are online worldwide; we'll
  find a slot that works for both of us.
