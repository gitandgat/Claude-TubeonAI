## Gig video script (~60 seconds, ~140 words)
Works as a talking-head (you read to camera) OR as narration/captions over an
animated render.

**[0–8s — hook]**
Have you ever looked at a life you worked really hard for and thought… this
doesn't feel like mine anymore?

**[8–20s — credibility]**
I'm Sahawat. I'm a former physician. I spent years becoming a doctor — and then
I walked away from the whole identity I'd built. So I know that disorientation
from the inside.

**[20–40s — what it is]**
That's why I do Identity Audits. Together we strip away the expectations that were
never yours, find where your energy is leaking, and reconnect with what actually
pulls you forward. No hustle clichés. No worksheets. Just honest, grounded
conversation.

**[40–55s — who it's for / outcome]**
If you're standing at the edge of a transition, unsure who you are now — you'll
leave with real clarity on your next step.

**[55–60s — CTA]**
Send me a message. Let's figure out what your next chapter looks like.

### Recording guide (if talking-head)
- Phone is fine, shot at eye level, window light in front of you.
- Quiet room, look into the lens (not the screen).
- Warm and calm > polished. Buyers want to feel you, not a TV ad.
- One clean take per section; stitch later. ~60s total, keep it under 75s.
