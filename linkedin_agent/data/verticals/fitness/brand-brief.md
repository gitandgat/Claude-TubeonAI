## Customer
Lifters and active adults who keep getting hurt or stalling and are tired of bro-science. They want the physiology behind the cue, why a tweak fixes pain, why glutes matter, what "recovery" actually does. Smart, skeptical, want evidence not hype.

## Primary CTA

## Strong Opinion / Wedge
Most strength advice is bro-science dressed as wisdom. Sore isn't injured. "Feeling the burn" isn't progress. The warm-up everyone skips matters more than the program everyone copies. And glute strength changes how a person moves through the world more than any aesthetic goal admits. A clinician can say what gym culture won't.
