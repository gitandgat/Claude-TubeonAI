# Brand Brief: trainer (clinician-turned-PT, Ottawa, hiring play)

> Captured: 2026-06-18 (seeded from verticals.py registry + brand voice memory)
> LinkedIn slot 11:00 ET. Persona: Dr. Sahawat. This vertical is the visibility/hiring play.

## Business
Dr. Sahawat, a physician who left clinical medicine and is building a personal-training practice in Ottawa, in the open. The work: clinical-grade coaching (biomechanics, injury prevention, metabolic physiology). The goal of this vertical is visibility to gym owners, hiring managers, prospective clients, and people to build with.

## Customer
Two readers at once: (1) Ottawa gym owners / fitness + healthcare hiring managers who could bring on a clinically-trained coach; (2) prospective clients, adults who keep getting hurt or plateauing and want someone who understands the body, not just reps. Both value a clinician who can explain the "why."

## Primary CTA
"I'm building a personal training practice in Ottawa with a clinical background in biomechanics and injury prevention. Open to gym connections, clients, and PT or medical-adjacent roles, connect or DM me." (first comment).

## Strong Opinion / Wedge
A clinical background makes a better coach than another certification. Most trainers chase certs and treat symptoms (sore muscles, quick programs). A clinician trains the cause, load, tissue tolerance, biomechanics, prevention. The fitness industry over-values credentials it invented and under-values the one that took 12 years.
