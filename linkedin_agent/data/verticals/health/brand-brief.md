## Strong Opinion / Wedge
Prevention beats prescription, and the system is structurally too rushed to deliver it. A 10-minute walk after meals and consistent strength training outperform a lot of what gets prescribed, but nobody's reimbursed to say so in a 7-minute visit. The most underrated health intervention isn't a pill; it's load-bearing movement.
