## VISUAL STYLE GUIDE FOR REMOTION

### Composition Specifications
- **Format:** 1080×1920 (vertical, for social/email)
- **Duration:** 5-10 minutes total
- **Color Palette:** Match Crosswalk Wisdom brand (ambers, charcoals, warmwhite)
- **Font:** Playfair Display (headlines), Inter (body text)
- **Motion:** Subtle, intentional (no cheesy transitions)

### Elements
```
1. Title Card (0:00-1:00)
   - Large headline
   - Your name/brand
   - Video number (1/3, 2/3, 3/3)

2. Animated Graphics (throughout)
   - Framework diagrams
   - Process flows
   - Results visualization
   - Data/stats

3. B-Roll Footage (optional)
   - Could film yourself
   - Or use stock footage from Unsplash/Pexels
   - Keep it professional, not cheesy

4. Text Overlays
   - Key points emphasized
   - Social proof (if applicable)
   - Step-by-step breakdown

5. End Card (final 10s)
   - CTA button/link
   - Next video preview
   - Subscribe prompt
```

### Pacing
- **Video 1 (5 min):** Slower pace, hook-focused
- **Video 2 (6-8 min):** Medium pace, educational
- **Video 3 (8-10 min):** Builds urgency toward end

## Production Tips

### Script Delivery
- Record yourself, not a voiceover artist (more authentic)
- Speak naturally, not from a script (but use these as guides)
- Record in 2-3 takes, choose the best one
- Aim for conversational tone, not formal/sales-y

### Editing
- Keep cuts clean and intentional
- Use Remotion to sync graphics with narration
- Add subtle music (copyright-free, like Epidemic Sound)
- Color-grade to match brand palette
- Add captions/subtitles (for accessibility + sound-off viewing)

### Length
- Don't pad to fill time
- If your message is done in 4 minutes, send a 4-minute video
- These script times are estimates, adjust for your pacing

### Quality
- Minimum: 1080p resolution
- Audio: Clean, no background noise
- Thumbnail: Compelling enough to click on in email

---

## Alternative: AI-Generated Video

If you prefer not to record yourself:

1. Use a tool like HeyGen or Synthesia to generate AI voiceover
2. Record yourself reading the script once (even roughly)
3. Use Remotion to generate all graphics/animations
4. Result: Professional video without hiring a video editor

Cost: ~$200-500 per video vs $1000-2000 for freelancer
