# Cold Outreach Kit — AI Consultant Validation
Ready to copy-paste into Sendpilot (or manual LinkedIn search). Goal: 10 interviews using the 7 questions in LINKEDIN_RESEARCH_STRATEGY.md.

---

## 1. Sendpilot search filters

Use these in Sendpilot's lead database / search:

- **Title:** Owner OR Founder OR CEO OR Managing Director OR President
- **Industry:** Marketing & Advertising, Design Services, Public Relations, Digital Marketing
- **Company size:** 11-50 employees (covers your 5-30 target with some buffer)
- **Geography:** Start with English-speaking markets you can call easily — US, Canada, UK, Australia (adjust to your timezone)
- **Exclude:** Freelancers / solo (1-10 employees often = no team, no operational drag to solve)

Pull a list of 40-50. You won't message all of them — see daily plan below.

---

## 2. DM templates (3 variants — rotate so messages don't look templated)

All variants: short, no pitch, specific ask, easy to say yes to.

### Variant A — Curiosity/Research framing
```
Hey [Name] — saw you run [Agency Name]. I'm doing some research on how
agency owners are using (or not using) AI in day-to-day operations —
proposals, reporting, that kind of thing.

Would you be open to a quick 15-min call? Genuinely just trying to
understand what's working and what's not. No pitch.

Free this week or next?
```

### Variant B — Specific pain point hook
```
Hi [Name] — quick one. I keep hearing from agency owners that client
reporting and proposals eat way more time than they'd like.

Curious if that's true for [Agency Name] too, or if you've already
solved it. Doing some informal research — happy to share what I'm
learning if useful.

Got 15 min for a quick chat sometime?
```

### Variant C — Peer/collaborator framing
```
Hey [Name] — I'm putting together a small project documenting how
agencies are actually getting ROI from AI (or struggling to). Looking
for 8-10 agency owners willing to share their experience, confidentially.

Would love 15 minutes if you're open to it — no sales angle, just
trying to get the real picture.
```

**Personalization rule:** always swap in their actual agency name and, if you can see it, one specific detail from their profile/posts (e.g., "saw your post about scaling client work" or "noticed [Agency Name] does a lot of [niche]"). One sentence of real personalization roughly doubles reply rates.

---

## 3. Daily action plan (15-20 min/day)

- Send **8-10 connection requests/DMs per day** (LinkedIn's own soft limits are roughly 15-20/day for newer accounts — staying under 10 keeps you well clear of restrictions)
- Rotate templates A/B/C so it doesn't look automated
- Spend 5 min replying to anyone who responds — book the call immediately while they're warm
- Track everything in a simple list: name, agency, date messaged, template used, response (yes/no/no reply)

At 8-10/day with a realistic 10-20% positive response rate, expect your first "yes" within 2-3 days and 10 interviews booked within 2-3 weeks — much faster than waiting on the LinkedIn post queue alone.
