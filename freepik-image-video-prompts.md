# Freepik Image + Video Prompts — All Posts

## How to use

1. Go to **freepik.com/ai** → **Image Generator**
2. Paste the **Image Prompt** → Select **Square 1:1** → Generate
3. Pick the best result → Click **Image to Video** (or go to freepik.com/ai/video-generator)
4. Paste the **Video Motion Prompt** → Generate (5-10 second clip)
5. Download both the image and video
6. Drop files in: `/crosswalk-remotion/public/assets/`

Recommended video model: **Kling 2.1** or **Runway Gen 4** (best for subtle cinematic motion)

## Tips for Best Results

- **Model choice:** Use Kling 2.1 or Runway Gen 4 for subtle, cinematic motion. Avoid models that add too much movement.
- **Duration:** 5 seconds is ideal for social posts. Instagram Reels auto-loop short videos.
- **Aspect ratio:** Generate images as **square 1:1** (1080x1080) for Instagram/Facebook. For TikTok/Reels, generate a second version as **9:16 vertical**.
- **Style consistency:** Every prompt includes "35mm film grain, warm amber golden tones, cinematic" to maintain the Crosswalk Wisdom brand look.
- **Export:** Download as MP4. Zernio accepts video via the same presigned URL upload flow.
