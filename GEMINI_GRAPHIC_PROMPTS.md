## GRAPHIC #3: The Math
**For Post #3 (June 17)**

```
Create a professional LinkedIn post graphic for a business consultant.

Theme: Financial Impact - Annual Savings

Design Elements:
- Large, prominent number in center
- Supporting calculation below
- Professional financial aesthetic

Main Content:
- Huge, bold number: "$18,000" - Green color (#16A34A)
- Size: 140pt or larger
- Subheading: "What You're Leaving on the Table" - 32pt, below the number
- Calculation breakdown:
  5 hours/week × $75/hour = $375/week
  $375/week × 52 weeks = $19,500/year (approximately $18,000)

Text:
- "Imagine if one senior person on your team saved 5 hours per week on reporting..."
- Small text: "This is not a prediction. This is happening right now in your agency."

Colors:
- Background: Dark professional navy (#1E3A5F) or dark gray (#374151)
- Main number "$18,000": Bright success green (#16A34A)
- Supporting text: White (#FFFFFF)
- Accent elements: Light green highlights

Style: Bold, motivational, professional
Format: LinkedIn optimized (1200×627px)
```
