## Filming guide

**Camera:** Phone is fine. Shoot **horizontal (landscape), 1080p or 4K, 30fps**. (I can crop a 9:16 vertical cut for IG/TikTok after.)

**Framing:** Chest-up. Lens at **eye height**, eyes on the lens (not the screen). A little headroom. Gym or clean background.

**Lighting:** Face a window or soft light. Never a window/bright source *behind* you.

**Audio (most important):** Quiet, non-echoey room. Best: a lapel mic or earbuds mic. If using the phone mic, stay close (~0.5–1 m). Kill AC hum / fans.

**Delivery:** Leave ~3s of silence at the start and end of each take. Use a teleprompter app, or tape bullet notes right beside the lens. Energy slightly above "normal conversation."

**B-roll (optional, but it lifts the video):** 10–20s clips of you or a client doing hip thrusts / RDLs / glute bridges with clean form; the gym; any honest before/after. Send whatever you have.
