**Audience:** IMGs in Canada

## A) Hook Variants

**Hook A — Data reveal / hard truth**
> The Canadian residency match rate is 10–22%.
> They don't tell you that before you move countries.

*Why it works:* Leads with math (IMGs trust data). The second line lands the immigration layer immediately. Cognitive dissonance: the system withheld the odds.

---

**Hook B — Vulnerability contrast / sunk cost math**
> I calculated how much I'd spent chasing Canadian residency.
> Then I stopped counting.

*Why it works:* Personal, specific, creates dread-curiosity. The act of stopping counting is itself a story. Invites the reader to count their own.

---

**Hook C — Multi-layer cost + contrarian challenge (STRONGEST)**
> You've lost $80,000. Five years. Your accent. Maybe your marriage.
> Here's why "keep going" is the most dangerous advice anyone can give you.

*Why it works:* Names all 4 sunk cost layers in two lines (financial, time, identity, immigration/relationships). Immediately challenges the dominant cultural script. Creates maximum cognitive dissonance in the first breath.

---

**Hook D — Status-drop / identity contrast**
> I moved to Canada as a doctor.
> The licensing board didn't care.

*Why it works:* Sharp, short, devastating. The contrast between earned identity and institutional indifference captures the identity layer instantly.
