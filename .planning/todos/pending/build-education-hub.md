### Problem
Crosswalk Wisdom has no free educational content layer to attract and nurture healthcare professionals experiencing burnout. OffScript uses a free education hub (videos, articles) as their primary top-of-funnel engine — visitors get value before ever hitting a paywall. Currently, Crosswalk Wisdom relies solely on a PDF lead magnet with no surrounding content ecosystem to drive organic discovery or build trust at scale.

### Solution
Build an education hub on crosswalkwisdom.com modeled after OffScript's approach:

1. **Content pillars** — Map to the Crosswalk Method stages (Start/Stop/Elder/Human):
   - Burnout recognition & self-assessment articles
   - Healing framework explainers (video + written)
   - Transformation stories from healthcare workers
   - Burnout statistics & research roundups (feeds the existing /burnout-stats page)

2. **Formats** — Mix of short-form video (repurposed via TubeonAI pipeline), blog posts, and carousel graphics (already have Instagram carousel generation)

3. **Distribution** — Repurpose each piece across LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube Shorts using the existing TubeonAI content pipeline

4. **Conversion path** — Each piece ends with CTA to the 5 Crosswalk Lessons PDF → email nurture sequence → coaching offer

5. **SEO play** — Written content fixes the JS rendering/indexation problem flagged in the marketing audit by giving Google crawlable, keyword-rich pages
