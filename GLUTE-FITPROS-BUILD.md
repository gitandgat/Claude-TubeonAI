# Glute Longevity → FitPros.io Build Pack (in-person / GoodLife)

The low-effort way to build the posture-corrective glute program in **FitPros** (free, unlimited clients). FitPros builds with **drag-and-drop + a progression builder**, so you build **Week 1 only (3 workouts)** and auto-generate Weeks 2–6. Companion to the reusable engine in [GLUTE-POSTURE-AI-PROMPTS.md](GLUTE-POSTURE-AI-PROMPTS.md).

**Why FitPros here:** salaried at GoodLife → no payments/scheduling needed (GoodLife handles those; Stripe for any rare direct charge). FitPros gives free *unlimited* clients + 1,000+ HD demo videos + progression builder. Confirm GoodLife allows a third-party client app before relying on it.

## Step 1 — Account + program shell
1. Create the free account at fitpros.io (no card).
2. **Workout Builder → New Program** → name **Glute Longevity — 6-Week Protocol**, length **6 weeks**, **3 days/week**.

## Step 3 — Auto-generate Weeks 2–6 (progression builder)
Apply the program across 6 weeks, then set progression:
- **Strength lifts** (Glute Bridge, Box Squat, Hip Hinge, Step Up): **+1–2 reps/week** to Week 4, then add load/tempo.
- **Correctives & core** (pull-apart, face pull, dead bug, cobra, planks, chin tuck): **+5s hold or +2 reps/week**.
- This gives a clean linear progression with **zero extra building**.

## Step 4 (optional) — Variety swaps for a stronger arc
If you want the designed variety (not just linear), edit these 3 spots only:
- **Week 3:** Box Squat → **Goblet Squat**; add **Hip Thrust** in place of one bridge (load phase).
- **Week 4:** swap one move on B/C for **Suitcase Carry** (posture/carry phase).
- **Week 5:** make the main squat **Tempo Goblet Squat 4×8 (4-1-1)**; add **Pallof Press** (power/anti-lordosis phase).

## Step 5 — Deliver to a client
1. Add the client (free, unlimited) → assign the program.
2. They follow on the FitPros client app (installs to phone home screen) — each move has an HD demo + your cues, and they log sets.
3. You coach live at the gym; the app is what they follow between sessions and how you track progress.
