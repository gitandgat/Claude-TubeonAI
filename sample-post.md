# Sample Post for Testing

Here's what nobody really tells you about the white coat.

Leaving medicine is incredibly difficult. The reasons are structural and deeply rooted in how we're trained. Professional identity becomes fused with personal identity, and honestly, the anxiety about losing status is completely understandable.

This is the research talking. Studies clearly show that physicians experience something called identity threat when considering transitions. As it turns out, the emotional impact is significant. The truth is that staying, even when it's destroying you, feels safer than the alternative. Everything we've been taught reinforces this.

But here's the thing — you don't have to abandon your physician identity entirely. You can expand it fundamentally.
