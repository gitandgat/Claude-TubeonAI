# How to Sell the Demand Discovery System

You built something valuable. People will pay for this. Here are 5 ways to monetize it.

---

## Model 1: SaaS ($29-99/month)

**Product:** "Opportunity Scanner" - web app that finds opportunities daily

**How it works:**
- User logs in to dashboard
- Sees daily opportunities scored and ranked
- Pre-generated messages ready to send
- Tracks conversations + revenue pipeline
- Exports to CSV

**Revenue:**
- $29/month (Starter) — 100 opportunities/month, basic tracking
- $79/month (Pro) — unlimited opportunities, advanced tracking, email alerts
- $199/month (Agency) — white-label, team members, API access

**Time to launch:** 3-4 weeks (build web interface, add auth)

**Realistic MRR:** 
- 50 customers × $49 average = $2,450/month
- 500 customers × $49 average = $24,500/month

**Who buys:**
- Freelancers (need consistent leads)
- Agencies (bulk outreach)
- Solopreneurs (passive discovery)
- Content creators (partnership opportunities)

---

## Model 2: Done-For-You Service ($500-5000/month)

**Service:** "I run discovery FOR you, send outreach, manage conversations"

**How it works:**
1. You run the system daily for each client
2. You generate opportunities + messages
3. You send initial outreach on THEIR behalf (with their voice)
4. You track conversations
5. You nurture leads until they're ready to close
6. Client closes the deals themselves
7. You get paid monthly for the pipeline management

**Pricing:**
- $500-999/month (Solo) — 10-15 opportunities/week, basic outreach
- $1500-2999/month (Growth) — 25-50 opportunities/week, full conversation management
- $5000+/month (Agency) — Dedicated account manager, multiple platforms, full lead nurturing

**Revenue:**
- 10 clients × $1000 average = $10,000/month
- 20 clients × $1500 average = $30,000/month

**Who buys:**
- Agencies (want done-for-you lead generation)
- Coaches/consultants (don't want to do outreach)
- Course creators (need steady leads)
- Service providers (don't have time)

**Effort:** 5-10 hours/week per client once streamlined

---

## Model 3: Agency Model ($2000-10000 per project)

**Service:** "I find your customers, you close them"

**How it works:**
1. Client tells you their service
2. You run discovery for 2-4 weeks
3. You generate 50-100 high-quality leads
4. You send initial outreach (them reaching out from YOUR account)
5. You deliver the warm leads to them
6. They close and get the customers
7. You take 20-30% of their first month revenue

**Example deal:**
```
Client: LinkedIn content creator wanting to sell services
You: Run discovery, find 50 people asking for LinkedIn content
You: Send messages like "I help with this" (positioning as them)
Outcome: 10 people interested, 3 close at $1500/month each = $4500/month
Your cut: 30% of first month = $1350 + ongoing 10% = $450/month
```

**Revenue:**
- 5 clients × $1350 first month commission = $6,750
- Plus 5 clients × $450 ongoing = $2,250/month

**Who buys:**
- Freelancers wanting to productize services
- Experts wanting to start business
- Agencies expanding into new service areas

---

## Model 4: Training/Course ($97-497)

**Product:** "How to Build Your Own Demand Discovery System"

**What they get:**
- All the code (discovery scripts)
- Step-by-step setup guide
- Message template generation system
- Conversation tracker
- Daily automation setup
- Video training
- Community access

**Pricing:**
- $97 (Basic) — Code + docs + email support
- $297 (Pro) — Includes 4-week coaching + group calls
- $497 (Agency) — White-label + team training + implementation support

**Revenue:**
- 100 students × $97 = $9,700
- 50 students × $297 = $14,850
- 20 students × $497 = $9,940
- Total: $34,590 (one-time)

**Recurring:**
- Community membership: $19-49/month
- Implementation support: $500 setup fee
- Expected MRR from community: 50 members × $29 = $1,450/month

**Who buys:**
- Solopreneurs wanting passive discovery
- Agencies wanting to automate outreach
- Consultants wanting to systematize lead gen
- Anyone selling services online

---

## Model 5: White-Label ($1000-5000 setup + revenue share)

**Product:** License your system to agencies/platforms

**How it works:**
- You build the system
- An agency/platform white-labels it (their branding)
- They sell it to their clients
- You get revenue share (30-50%)

**Example:**
- Agency wants to offer "Lead Discovery Tool" to clients
- You set it up for them
- They charge clients $50/month
- You get $15-25/month per user
- If they have 100 customers: $1500-2500/month for you (passive)

**Revenue:**
- 5 partners × 100 customers × $20 per user = $10,000/month

**Who buys:**
- Marketing agencies
- Freelance platforms
- Coaching platforms
- SaaS tools for creators
