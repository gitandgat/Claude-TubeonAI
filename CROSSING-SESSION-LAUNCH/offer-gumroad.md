## Product description (paste into Gumroad)

**A 60-minute call that replaces two years of "maybe next year."**

You've read the posts. You've felt the nod of recognition — the identity cage,
the cost of staying, the curb you've been standing on. Reading was the easy part.

The Crossing Session is one hour, one-on-one, on your actual situation:

- **Before the call** you take the 3-minute Fear Audit, so we start with a map of
  which fear — money, judgment, or identity — is really running your decisions.
- **On the call** we separate the real risks from the inherited ones, name what
  staying is costing you, and work out your first concrete step across.
- **Within 48 hours** you get a one-page Crossing Plan: your situation, your
  constraint, your next three moves, in writing.

**Who it's for:** healthcare professionals who keep deciding to decide later.
Nurses, physicians, allied health — anyone whose title has quietly become a cage.

**Who it's not for:** anyone looking for someone to tell them to quit. That's not
what this is. Some people leave the call with a plan to stay — on purpose, with
the fear named and priced. That counts as a crossing too.

**The guarantee:** if you finish the call without more clarity than you came with,
say so and I'll refund the full $97. You've spent more than that on textbooks you
never opened.

**Founding rate:** the first 10 sessions are $97. After that it's $147.

*From the Ward to the World.*
