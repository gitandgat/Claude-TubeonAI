# Daily outreach kit — 10 conversations a day, 20 minutes

The math: 10/day × 25 days = 250 conversations. At a 4% close rate that's 10 sessions = $970+.
This is the engine. The posts and emails are air support.

**The one rule:** every message is written for one specific person, references something
real about them, and asks a question. No links in the first message. Track everything
in `outreach-tracker.csv`.

---

## Where to find your 10 (in priority order)

1. **Your post engagers** — open LinkedIn notifications, list everyone who commented or
   reacted in the last 48h. Warmest people on the platform.
2. **Email repliers and quiz takers** — anyone who replies to the launch emails or shows
   up in Encharge with a `fearType` set. They've raised their hand twice.
3. **Your Facebook group members** — facebook.com/groups/2073825613397240. Welcome new
   members personally, by name.
4. **Commenters on adjacent creators' posts** — people writing "this is so me" under
   physician-burnout / nurse-burnout posts from bigger accounts.
5. **Dormant connections** — healthcare people already in your network you've never
   actually spoken to.

## The structure (Hormozi's ACA)

- **Acknowledge** — something specific they said or did
- **Compliment** — genuine, about them, not flattery
- **Ask** — one question about their situation

Then listen. The quiz comes second message. The session offer comes only after they've
shared a real struggle — usually message three or four.

---

## Openers by scenario

**1. Commented on your post:**
> Your comment on my post about [topic] stuck with me — especially "[their phrase]".
> Sounds like you've been carrying this a while. What's keeping you at that particular curb?

**2. Reacted but didn't comment:**
> Noticed you've been reacting to a few of my posts about leaving healthcare. I'm curious —
> what's your situation? Considering a move, or already mid-crossing?

**3. Replied to a launch email:**
> Thanks for writing back — most people just read and file it away. You mentioned
> [their words]. How long has that been the situation?

**4. Took the Fear Audit (visible in Encharge):**
> Saw you took the Fear Audit — you scored highest on [fearType]. That one's sneaky
> because it dresses up as prudence. Did the result match what you expected?

**5. New Facebook group member:**
> Welcome to the group, [name]. Everyone here is somewhere on the same crosswalk —
> mind if I ask what brought you in? Burnout, a pivot you're weighing, or just curious?

**6. Engaged with a bigger creator's burnout post:**
> Saw your comment on [creator]'s post about [topic]. "[their phrase]" — that line could
> be the title of half the conversations I have. What kind of medicine/nursing are you in?

**7. Dormant connection in healthcare:**
> We connected a while back and never actually talked. I write about career crossings
> for healthcare people now — how are things on your end of the ward?

## Follow-ups

**After they share a struggle → quiz:**
> What you're describing — [reflect it back in one line] — usually traces to one of three
> fears: money, judgment, or identity. I built a free 3-minute audit that tells you which
> one is driving. Want the link?

**After quiz / deeper conversation → session (the only pitch):**
> I'll be straight with you: this is exactly what my Crossing Sessions are for. One hour
> on your actual situation, and you leave with a written plan — your constraint, your
> next three moves. I'm doing the first ten at $97 (founding rate), refund if you don't
> leave clearer. No pressure either way — happy to keep talking here regardless. Want the link?

**No reply after 4–5 days (one nudge max):**
> No worries if this got buried — one thing I meant to ask: [new specific question].
