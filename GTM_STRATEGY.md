### Channel 3: Partnerships (Scalable)

**Other people promote your system to their audiences**

**Target Partners:**

1. **LinkedIn Creator Communities**
   - Find creators with 10K-100K followers
   - "I have a system that generates leads for your audience"
   - Revenue split: 20-30% commission

2. **Agency Owners**
   - They want white-label tools to sell clients
   - Offer: $1000 setup + 30% revenue share
   - Expected: 10-50 customers per partner

3. **Coaching Platforms**
   - (Creator communities, Teachable, Mighty Networks)
   - They want to offer lead gen to members
   - Revenue: $1000-5000 per platform partnership

4. **Content Creator Groups**
   - Discord communities, Slack groups, newsletters
   - Pay them $500-1000 for promotion
   - Each partnership = 5-20 customers

5. **Freelance Communities**
   - Indie Hackers, Product Hunt, StartupSchool
   - Post updates + get organic discovery

**Partner outreach template:**
```
"Hi [Partner],

I built a system that automatically finds 50-100 
pre-qualified leads for service businesses.

Your audience (agencies/coaches/creators) 
would benefit from this.

I'd pay you $500 to recommend it to your 
community + 20% ongoing revenue share.

Interest in chatting?"
```

**Expected results:**
- 5 partnerships = 50-100 customers
- $25K-100K additional revenue

**Effort:** 2-5 hours/week
