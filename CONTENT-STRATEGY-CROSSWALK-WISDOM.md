# Crosswalk Wisdom — Content Strategy

**Brand:** Crosswalk Wisdom by Dr. Sahawat Nilwatcharamanee
**Voice:** First-person, vulnerable, psychologically grounded, direct
**Lead Magnet:** Fear Audit (https://fear-audit.vercel.app/) — free 5-min quiz
**Paid Product:** The Courage to Choose ($27 PDF on Gumroad)
**Funnel:** Social content → Fear Audit quiz → Email nurture (5 emails) → Courage to Choose purchase

---

## Business Context

Sahawat is a former physician (Thailand) who left medicine after burnout, moved to Canada, and became a crossing guard. He now coaches stuck professionals through career transitions. His unique angle: he didn't pivot to another prestigious career — he chose peace over prestige, and built wisdom from that choice.

**ICP (Ideal Customer Profile):**
- Professionals aged 28-50 in high-prestige, high-burnout careers (medicine, law, finance, consulting, academia)
- Feeling trapped by identity, sunk cost, social expectations
- Know they want out but can't name what's stopping them
- Often women (74% of women physicians have considered career change per 2026 Doximity data)
- English-speaking, globally distributed (Thailand, US, Canada, UK, Australia)

**Primary Goal:** Lead generation (Fear Audit completions) → paid product sales

## Competitive Landscape

### Direct Competitors

| Competitor | Approach | Gap |
|-----------|----------|-----|
| Happen To Your Career | Podcast + coaching. Strengths-based. Broad audience | No emotional/fear framework. Generic. No personal story anchor |
| Jenny Blake (Pivot) | Book + speaking. Strengths-first pivoting | Focuses on "what next" not "what's stopping you." No fear taxonomy |
| Ken Coleman | Radio show + books. Proximity Principle | Corporate/Christian audience. No burnout or identity crisis angle |
| Careershifters | UK-based. Courses + community | Process-oriented, lacks raw personal narrative |
| Kim Kimball Coaching | Midlife career change blog | Good SEO content but no lead magnet funnel, no unique framework |

### Crosswalk Wisdom's Unfair Advantage
1. **The story** — MD → crossing guard is unforgettable, shareable, pattern-interrupting
2. **The framework** — 5 Fears taxonomy + 4 Stages of Stuck = ownable intellectual property
3. **The tool** — Fear Audit is interactive, personalized, and creates data for segmentation
4. **The voice** — first-person, lived experience, psychologically grounded (not corporate coach-speak)

### Content Gaps in the Market
- Nobody is naming the **5 specific fears** (Financial, Identity, Judgment, Failure, Grief) as a framework
- "Career identity crisis" has high emotional resonance but thin content — mostly therapist blogs, no personal story-driven content
- **Grief of leaving** is massively underserved — most content jumps to "here's how to pivot" without honoring the mourning
- **The gap between leaving and landing** — almost no content addresses the quiet in-between period
- **Sunk cost as emotional trap** (not just logical fallacy) — NIH wrote about it but no personal brand owns it

## Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: THE FEAR (Awareness)
**What it is:** Name and normalize the specific fears holding people in careers that are breaking them.
**Why:** This is the entry point. People searching "afraid to quit my job" or "career identity crisis" land here.
**Connects to:** Fear Audit (lead magnet)

Topic Cluster:
```
THE FEAR
├── The 5 Fears Inside "I Can't Leave"
│   ├── Financial Fear: Running the actual numbers
│   ├── Identity Fear: Who am I without this title?
│   ├── Judgment Fear: What will people think?
│   ├── Failure Fear: What if it doesn't work?
│   └── Grief Fear: What if I wasted everything?
├── The 4 Stages of Stuck (START, STOP, ELDER, HUMAN)
├── Why naming your fear changes everything
├── The Fear Audit: what it is and how it works
└── "I can't leave" — unpacking the sentence
```

### Pillar 2: THE STORY (Shareable)
**What it is:** Sahawat's personal narrative — medicine, burnout, the psychiatrist, the crossing guard, the yellow vest.
**Why:** This is the viral engine. The story is the brand. Every platform encounter should eventually lead back to it.
**Connects to:** Brand awareness → Fear Audit

Topic Cluster:
```
THE STORY
├── MD to Crossing Guard (origin story)
├── The psychiatrist who said "the system is the system"
├── The phone call with my father
├── Why I chose peace over prestige
├── The first morning in the yellow vest
├── What the children taught me about identity
└── Letters from the crosswalk (ongoing series)
```

### Pillar 3: THE GRIEF (Consideration)
**What it is:** The emotional reality of leaving — mourning, silence, doubt, the "gap."
**Why:** This is the deepest content. Nobody else is writing about this honestly. Massive search + share potential.
**Connects to:** Courage to Choose (paid product)

Topic Cluster:
```
THE GRIEF
├── 5 things nobody tells you about leaving a "good" career
├── The first 6 months are the loneliest
├── Grieving a career you chose to leave
├── The 3am questions ("Did I throw it all away?")
├── How to survive the gap between leaving and landing
├── When your friends take it personally
└── You are allowed to mourn something you chose to end
```

### Pillar 4: THE REBUILD (Decision/Implementation)
**What it is:** What comes after — not "follow your passion" but practical, grounded next steps.
**Why:** Converts readers who've done the Fear Audit into buyers of Courage to Choose.
**Connects to:** Courage to Choose → coaching inquiries

Topic Cluster:
```
THE REBUILD
├── You don't need a plan, you need a first step
├── How to answer "what do you do?" when you don't know anymore
├── The difference between commitment and captivity
├── Why your old career wasn't a waste (reframing sunk cost)
├── 7 Crosswalk Reflections for Recovering Overachievers
├── What I learned from starting over at zero
└── The Courage to Choose: what's in the book and who it's for
```

## Content Format Strategy by Platform

### LinkedIn (3x/week — Mon/Wed/Fri 9am ET)
- Long-form text posts (800-1500 characters) + square image
- Professional but vulnerable. First person. Story-driven.
- CTA: "Fear Audit — free, 5 minutes. [link]" or "Link in comments"
- No hashtags (reduce reach for personal brands on LinkedIn)

### Instagram (3x/week — same schedule)
- Square image + condensed caption + 8 hashtags
- Slightly punchier. Same warmth. More line breaks.
- CTA: "Link in bio"
- Core hashtags: #careerchange #fearaudit #crosswalkwisdom #burnout #identitycrisis #courage #personalgrowth

### Facebook (3x/week — same schedule)
- Mid-length text + square image + direct link
- Same as LinkedIn but slightly warmer
- CTA: Direct link to Fear Audit in post body

### TikTok (2x/week — when videos rendered)
- Vertical video from Remotion compositions
- "Talking to one person" energy
- CTA: "Link in bio"

### YouTube Shorts (repurpose TikTok — 2x/week)
- Cross-post TikTok vertical videos
- Leverage YouTube as search engine
