## PRO TIPS

1. **Post from desktop, not mobile** - Scheduling works better on desktop
2. **Hashtags matter** - LinkedIn algorithm favors posts with 3-5 hashtags
3. **Ask a question** - Posts with questions get 2x more engagement
4. **Engagement window** - First 2 hours are critical; come back and reply to comments
5. **Best performing times** - Tue-Thu, 8-10am or 12-2pm ET (when people check LinkedIn at desk)
